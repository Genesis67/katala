About Katala Home Essentials

Elevating Nigerian homes, one piece at a time

Our story

Founded in 2020, Katala Home Essentials was born from a simple idea: create premium home and kitchen products that blend modern aesthetics with uncompromised quality. Every piece is designed with care and crafted with the finest materials.

We believe that your home should be a sanctuary — functional, beautiful, and inspiring. Our collection is inspired by the warmth of Nigerian hospitality, the richness of our culture, and the spirit of those who take pride in their homes.
