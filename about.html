About Katala Homes

Crafted with love in Italy

Our story

Founded in 2020, Katala Homes (Italian for "love") was born from a simple idea: create footwear that seamlessly blends modern aesthetics with uncompromised comfort. Every pair is designed in Milan and handcrafted with the finest Italian materials.

We believe that shoes should feel like a second skin — lightweight, responsive, and timeless. Our collection is inspired by the energy of the city, the rhythm of the streets, and the spirit of those who walk their own path with confidence.
